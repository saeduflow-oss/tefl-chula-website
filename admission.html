Admission

Admission

We welcome applicants who are committed to professional growth in English language teaching and education.

How to Apply

Admission Procedures

Applicants must meet all admission requirements before being considered. The application runs through both the TEFL program and the Graduate School.

Who Can Apply

Admission Requirements

The program has two codes: 4987 (Plan 1, Academic Track) and 4988 (Plan 2, Professional Track). General eligibility requires a bachelor’s degree related to English or linguistics, a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 on a 4.00 scale, and one of the following minimum English proficiency scores:

English Test Minimum Score
CU-TEP75
TOEFL iBT79
TOEFL ITP / PBT550
IELTSBand 6

Applicants with a bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution in a country where English is the primary language (e.g. the UK, USA, Australia, New Zealand, Canada) may be exempted from the English test requirement.
