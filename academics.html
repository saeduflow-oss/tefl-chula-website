Academics

Academic Program

Our academic structure is designed to support both theoretical understanding and practical training for future English language educators.

At a Glance

Overview

This is a two-year program aimed at both pre-service and in-service English teachers, offering study plans that combine research and coursework in different proportions to suit different student backgrounds.

Students have up to four academic years from admission to complete all requirements, and thesis-track students must have their research proposal approved within the first two years.

How It Works

Curriculum Info.

Five Things to Know

Enrollment

Register for at least 9 and no more than 15 credits per semester; fewer than 9 credits requires special permission.

Grading

Coursework A–F; thesis credits S/U each semester, then Very Good, Good, Pass or Fail after the defense. Keep a cumulative GPA of 3.00.

Thesis-Hour Enrollment

Consult your advisor each term, submit a work plan, and file a Work-in-Progress Report. Two consecutive U grades end student status.

Publication Requirement

Part of the thesis (Plans A/B) or the master project output (Plan C) must be published in a peer-reviewed journal or presented with full proceedings.

Comprehensive Exam

Non-thesis students may sit the exam after all four required courses and must pass before graduating, with one re-sit the following semester.

Choose Your Track

Study Plan

The current curriculum (B.E. 2568, for students admitted from academic year 2025 onward) offers two tracks:

Plan A
Academic Track
Coursework + Thesis

36 credit hours of coursework, plus a thesis — part of which must be published or presented at an academic conference.

 Plan B
Professional Track
Coursework + Project

33 credit hours of coursework, plus a 3-credit research-based master project and a comprehensive exam.

 Plan A
About the Thesis

Supervised independent research on an explicit theoretical foundation — problem, framework, methodology, findings and conclusions.

 Plan B
About the Master Project

More applied than a thesis — a tangible product plus a written report covering its basis, development and any trial results.

The prior curriculum (B.E. 2566) had three plans: Plan A (thesis only, no required coursework), Plan B (36 credits plus thesis), and Plan C (33 credits plus master project plus comprehensive exam).
