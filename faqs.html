Before You Apply

FAQs for Applicants

Common questions from prospective students, with short answers.

How do I apply?
Complete the online program application and the Graduate School application; see Admission Procedures for the full process.
When are the deadlines?
Generally two rounds a year — roughly Feb–Mar and September; exact dates are on the Admission Deadline page.
What documents are needed?
Transcripts, English test results, a statement of purpose, two recommendation letters, and other required documents.
Which English tests are accepted?
CU-TEP / TOEFL iBT / TOEFL ITP-PBT / IELTS, per the minimum scores listed.
Can I submit my English score later?
No — a valid score (issued within 2 years) must be submitted with the application.
What’s the minimum GPA?
2.75 on a 4.00 scale at the bachelor’s level.
What’s the application fee?
34 USD or 1,000 THB, non-refundable.
What study plans exist?
An Academic Track (thesis) and a Professional Track (master project plus comprehensive exam).
What are the estimated costs?
Around 65,500 THB/semester for Thai students and 105,200 THB/semester for non-Thai students, plus the application fee and comprehensive exam fee.
Is financial aid available for international students?
Yes — see Chulalongkorn University’s central scholarship pages for details.
Already Enrolled

FAQs for TEFL Students

Course registration questions from enrolled students, with short answers.

How do I register for courses?
Register via reg.chula.ac.th using your student ID.
Where can I find each semester’s schedule?
Each semester’s schedule is posted on the program’s Tentative Schedule page.
Can I add or drop a course after registering?
Yes. Courses can be added or dropped during the Add/Drop period set by the academic calendar.
What if a course is already full?
Contact the program office or the instructor about waitlisting.
Can I still register after the deadline?
Late registration is possible during the late period, subject to a penalty fee.
How do I withdraw from a course?
Course withdrawal follows the official procedure and deadlines in the registration manual.

Still Have Questions?

Reach the TEFL office by email or phone — we’re happy to help.
