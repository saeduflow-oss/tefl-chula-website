Research

Research

Research is a central component of the TEFL Program, helping students connect theory with practice and produce meaningful contributions to English language education.

What We Study

TEFL Research Overview

Student theses vary widely in the language skills addressed, the learner level, whether the focus is on teachers or learners, the type of intervention (e.g. new instructional processes or courses), and the research design — quantitative, qualitative, mixed-methods or R&D.

Recurring topics include communicative activities, web-based reading instruction, multiple-intelligences-based reading, media-based instruction, English accent variety, team-based learning, willingness to communicate, task-based reading instruction, self-directed learning, English counseling programs, inquiry-based reading instruction, drama-based instruction and locally-contextualized content.
