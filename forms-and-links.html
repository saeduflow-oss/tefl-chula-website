Student Services

Request Forms Submission Procedures

Students request forms via the Registrar’s Google Form; on submission, a PDF is emailed to the student’s university address. The student signs it, routes it to their advisor for approval by email, and then forwards the approved form to the program office for processing.

Research Support

Research Collaboration Letters

Students conducting research with schools or other institutions can request official letters supporting the collaboration. The request is submitted to the program office, which prepares the letter on the student’s behalf.

Finishing Up

Graduation Request

The graduation request covers the procedures and documents that must be submitted to the Faculty of Education in order to graduate, including the final thesis or master project deliverables.
